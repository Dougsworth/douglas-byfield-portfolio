        Can I bring food?

        Yes. Picnics are welcome, and there are jerk, patty and ice cream vendors on site. No glass on the lawn.

        Are the films suitable for children?

        The 7:00 pm screening each night is family friendly. Later films carry their own ratings in the programme.

        What if I lose my booking code?

        Your code is saved on this phone and sent to your WhatsApp number at checkout. The gate can also find you by name.

        Is there seating for wheelchair users?

        Row A at both ends is kept on firm decking with level access from the main path. Mention it at checkout.
